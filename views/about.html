About Us

We are NEXUS Club

NEXUS Club is a student-run technology and innovation community at APEX University, established in 2018. We bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world tech application through hands-on experiences, collaborative projects, and meaningful community engagement.

Join Our Community View Events
Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Mission

To foster a culture of continuous learning, technological innovation, and collaborative problem-solving among students. We empower every member to become a confident creator and contributor in the digital world.

Vision

To be the most impactful student technology community in the country, producing future-ready professionals and entrepreneurs who use technology as a force for positive change in society.

Objectives

Conduct workshops and hackathons, mentor students in emerging technologies, build industry partnerships, represent the university in national competitions, and develop real-world projects with social impact.

Our Story

How We Grew

2018

Foundation

NEXUS Club was founded by 12 passionate CS students who wanted a space to learn, build, and connect beyond the classroom.

2019

First Hackathon

Organized our debut 24-hour hackathon with 100+ participants, winning the university's Best Student Initiative award.

2021

Community Expansion

Membership grew to 300+ students across multiple departments. Launched monthly tech talk series with industry professionals.

2023

National Recognition

Won Best College Tech Club at the National Student Innovation Summit. Crossed 500 active members milestone.

2025

Today & Beyond

Expanding our impact with AI/ML projects, open-source contributions, and industry mentorship programs.
